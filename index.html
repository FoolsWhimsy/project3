Cerritos, My Hometown

Before I moved to Washington State, I grew up in Cerritos, California. The warm summers and mild winters are such a stark contrast to Washington's climate, which has more diverse weather. Cerritos is home to 45,579 people, mostly Asian and Hispanic, with a wide variety of delicious food to explore. If you want to enjoy the sunshine and go outside to meet your friends, Cerritos is the place to go!

City Facts

  • Population: approximately 45,579 residents
  • Located in Los Angeles County, southeastern California
  • Climate: Mediterranean — warm summers, mild winters
  • Land area: 8.73 square miles
  • Most spoken languages: English, Korean, Tagalog, Mandarin
  • Median household income: $135,439
  • Incorporated: April 24, 1956

Los Cerritos Center

Photo by DarkNight0917, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

The mall is perhaps the biggest source of revenue for the city, and one of the best places to hang out with people. The mall boasts a movie theatre, a myriad of fashion, tech, and toy shops, as well as generous options for restaurants. As a massive indoor shopping mall, the Los Cerritos Center mall is a must-see.

To see what the mall offers, you can visit their official website here.

Heritage Park

Photo by DarkNight0917, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

If you want to avoid the hustle and bustle of the mall, Heritage Park is located nearby. The park includes themed buildings with the appearance of a Bostonian village from the 1770s, a pirate ship, and a replica of the Old North Church. Ducks, turtles, and geese can be found residing in the moat surrounding the island. The park is peaceful and large enough that you can go for a long and enjoyable walk, soaking in the greenery.

Learn more about Heritage Park on the City of Cerritos website.

Cerritos Library

Photo by Coolcaesar, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

You'll recognize the Cerritos library off of its futuristic design, including an exterior of titanium panels. This was my favorite place to spend growing up. It has a massive catalogue of books, multiple floors, a children's area, theater, and law library. There's also a large model of a T. Rex skeleton inside the children's area. It's a really aesthetic place to visit if you want to settle in with a good book.

You can learn more about the library at their official website.

Cerritos Performance Center

Photo by Ally Union, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons. .

A beautiful entertainment and music venue located in Downtown Cerritos, the performance center hosts many shows, including opera, cabaret, jazz, dance, magic, drama, musicals, and comedy performances. It's extremely sophisticated in its design, with technologically advanced movable partitions that can divide one large room into multiple smaller rooms as well as a state of the art sound system and dance floor. It's an amazing place to see shows and has an iconic design. Absolutely beautiful.

To see what shows are upcoming, check out the official CCPA website.
